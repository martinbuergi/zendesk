Five Iron Golf unifies customer conversations with Zendesk Contact Center

As Five Iron Golf expanded to 30 locations, disconnected systems made it difficult to deliver consistent customer service. By unifying voice, email, and chat in Zendesk Contact Center, the company created a single source of truth for agents, streamlined onboarding, improved efficiency, and built a foundation for future AI-powered service.

Golf has been around for centuries. Five Iron Golf is giving the game a modern upgrade.

With venues in urban areas across the United States, Five Iron Golf combines cutting-edge golf simulators with food, drinks, and entertainment in a lively, no-dress-code atmosphere.

“It’s really all about making golf more accessible and more fun,” Bridget O’Sullivan, Human Resources Manager at Five Iron Golf, says. “It doesn’t matter if you’ve been playing golf your entire life or if you have never picked up a club, we just want you to come in and have a good time.”

Since opening its first location in New York City’s Flatiron District in 2017, Five Iron Golf has grown to 30 corporate locations across the United States, with additional franchise locations operating domestically and internationally.

Building a single source of truth

As Five Iron expanded, its customer service requirements grew increasingly sophisticated. To support its voice operations, the company initially deployed Local Measure Engage on Amazon Connect, bringing modern cloud functionality to its contact center. However, as the business scaled across channels, Five Iron’s agents were managing customer interactions across separate ticketing, voice and point-of-sale platforms.

To streamline these workflows and gain a complete, unified view of each customer, the team set out to bring all channels into a single workspace. Following Zendesk’s acquisition of Local Measure, which integrated advanced voice capabilities directly into the platform with Zendesk Contact Center, Five Iron Golf brought its entire service operation together within Zendesk.

“Previously, our voice and ticketing channels operated independently,” O’Sullivan says. “In 2025, we consolidated our entire customer service operation into Zendesk, finally getting a single platform where everything sits in the same place.”

The change transformed how Five Iron’s agents handled customer inquiries.

“Bringing calls, chat, and emails into one unified interface gave our agents better visibility into the customer history and interactions,” O’Sullivan says. “It makes us more consistent and efficient.”

At the center of that transformation was Zendesk, which gave agents a unified platform to manage customer interactions.

“I think the biggest thing is that our agents now have a single source of truth,” O’Sullivan says. “They know where to go. They’re able to work out of this one system.”

The benefits went beyond visibility. Zendesk reduced manual tracking, streamlined onboarding, and eliminated much of the administrative work that followed customer interactions.

“Zendesk has been really helpful in allowing us to build and use the tool in a way that best supports our organization,” O’Sullivan says. “It’s been very customizable. We were able to build it out exactly how we needed it.”

Delivering consistency during peak season

Five Iron Golf made the switch to Zendesk Contact Center in the summer of 2025. Most of Five Iron’s locations operate in colder-weather markets, making the period from October through April especially busy.

During the peak season with the new platform, Five Iron’s customers received faster, more consistent answers, regardless of which agent they spoke with. As routine requests increasingly became automated, agents had more time to focus on complex customer needs, helping Five Iron maintain customer satisfaction above 90 percent while reducing repeat contacts for common inquiries.

“We’ve been able to accurately answer more customers than ever before,” O’Sullivan says. “And our onboarding process has become much faster when we bring on additional agents during our busy season.”

Customers weren’t the only ones benefiting. Before Zendesk, location managers often spent valuable time answering calls and emails while simultaneously serving guests in person. By centralizing support operations, Five Iron freed local teams to focus on the customers standing directly in front of them and deliver the in-person experience that sets Five Iron Golf apart.

About Five Iron Golf

Five Iron’s support team includes 15 remote agents across the Philippines, Honduras, and Nigeria, along with two U.S.-based shift managers. Approximately 80 percent of customer inquiries arrive by phone, while the rest come through email and chat.

“The calls can be anything from booking requests to questions about memberships, leagues, and golf lessons,” O’Sullivan says. “We also do corporate events, birthday parties, all kinds of stuff.”

Before Zendesk, Five Iron’s customer history was scattered across separate voice and ticketing platforms. Agents routinely switched between systems while trying to piece together previous interactions. Now, calls, emails, and chats are unified in a single workspace, giving agents complete visibility into every customer conversation.

Creating a foundation for AI-powered service

As Five Iron continues to grow, AI will play an increasingly important role in helping agents work more efficiently while maintaining a high-quality customer experience.

“It’s already having a huge impact on the workforce and that’s just going to continue,” O’Sullivan says. “I hope that we can continue to use it the right way to make people more efficient.”

The company is exploring opportunities to expand its knowledge base, improve reporting, introduce SMS support, and leverage AI-powered capabilities that help agents quickly find accurate information and deliver more consistent responses.

At the same time, O’Sullivan believes the future of customer service depends on balancing automation with human expertise.

“I think ultimately for us, some sort of mix of both is going to be the best use of AI and human interaction,” she says. “People are important, too.”

As Five Iron Golf continues expanding its footprint, Zendesk provides a repeatable foundation that helps the company scale customer service without sacrificing the consistent experience that keeps customers coming back.
