RecargaPay transforms Support With Zendesk Copilot

RecargaPay, Brazil’s largest independent financial platform, uses Zendesk to scale support for more than 14 million customers. Its generative AI chatbot resolves 70 percent of interactions in one contact, while Copilot reduced chat occupancy time by 30 percent, improved agent confidence, and helped deliver faster and more consistent customer service.

The story was originally published in Portuguese and has been translated to English.

RecargaPay—Brazil’s largest independent financial platform—serves more than 14 million customers through a single app. Founded in 2010, the fintech helps individuals and small businesses invest, make payments, take out loans, purchase insurance, and earn money simply and securely.

But as RecargaPay scaled, its customer support operation struggled to keep up.

“Our tools had not kept pace,” says Natasha Caldas, Customer Experience Product Manager at RecargaPay. “The setup worked when our company was smaller—but it no longer met the needs of a constantly growing company.”

From fragmented support to a scalable, AI-powered operation

Today, a generative AI-powered chatbot integrated with Zendesk automatically resolves 70 percent of RecargaPay’s support interactions in a single contact. With Zendesk Copilot, average chat occupancy time has fallen by 30 percent, while support specialists’ perception of Copilot’s usefulness has increased by 45 percentage points. Following the relaunch, 80 percent of specialists saw a concrete, positive impact from the tool.

“Zendesk gave us a foundation designed to evolve,” Caldas says. “It enables us to build a sustainable, secure, and high-performing operation.”

Before Zendesk, RecargaPay’s support platform operated in silos. Email was handled offline, while regulatory and consumer-protection channels were managed through separate external portals.

“We didn’t have a complete view of our customers’ support experience,” Caldas says. “Managers made decisions based on slow, manual reports, while support specialists had to navigate between different tools to resolve a single ticket.”

RecargaPay now pairs intelligent self-service with specialized human support. The operation includes 20 internal specialists who focus on regulatory and critical channels, including the ombudsman’s office, the Central Bank, consumer-protection portals, and social media. An external BPO team of approximately 40 people provides 24/7 customer service through chat, voice, and email.

Putting AI to work alongside support specialists

Zendesk Copilot assists support specialists from the moment a ticket arrives. The specialist sends an immediate first response to meet the SLA and reassure the customer that their request is being handled. In the background, Copilot—Zendesk’s AI assistant—identifies the issue, locates the relevant procedure, and suggests a response and classification through Auto assist.

“Copilot suggests responses and classifications, while the support specialist reviews the recommendation, adjusts the tone, and adds customer-specific context,” Caldas explains. “The support specialist leads while Copilot supports every step.”

For example, a RecargaPay customer was automatically redirected to a personal account while trying to access a business account. Copilot identified the issue and suggested the appropriate response and categorization within seconds. The support specialist personalized the message and resolved the ticket. The customer gave the interaction a CSAT score of five.

“What matters most here is not just speed—it’s that accuracy was maintained,” Caldas says. “Copilot accelerated support without compromising quality.”

Giving AI the right foundation

RecargaPay also used Zendesk Guide to rewrite its internal procedures in a way that AI could interpret clearly.

“We used clear language, logical structure, and explicit criteria,” Caldas says. “This enabled Auto assist to correctly interpret the customer’s issue, recommend the right resolution without requiring the support specialist to search for information, and classify the contact.”

The new procedures helped support specialists handle more tickets in the same amount of time, increasing capacity without requiring the team to grow at the same rate as the business.

“Specialists can now handle more tickets in the same amount of time,” Caldas says. “And our customers receive faster service.”

Auto assist recommends responses based on documented procedures. As a result, the performance gap between support specialists has decreased.

“This means customers receive the same quality of support regardless of which support specialist handles the interaction,” Caldas says.

From resistance to confidence

The transition to AI initially created concern among support specialists. RecargaPay addressed that resistance by helping them understand how Copilot works, where it helps, and where human judgment remains essential.

“Copilot created more space for agents to do what they do best,” Caldas says. “They spend less time searching for information and more time solving customers’ problems.”

The transformation followed an important setback. During the initial pilot, average occupancy time with Auto assist enabled was 4.5 minutes longer than without it. RecargaPay paused Copilot for 100 percent of its teams and started again from scratch.

“What shaped our view of Zendesk was not the problem itself—it was what happened afterward,” Caldas says. “The Zendesk team did not disappear. Instead, they became actively involved in understanding what had gone wrong—and helping us find an alternative path.”

With Zendesk’s product experts and engineers involved, RecargaPay rewrote its procedures and relaunched Copilot with a new configuration.

Building a support experience that keeps improving

Looking ahead, RecargaPay is focused on making support more predictive.

“The best support experience is one that does not need to happen,” Caldas says. “Before thinking about how to provide better support, we need to continue focusing on how to launch frictionless products.”

When customers do need help, RecargaPay wants to identify and resolve friction as quickly as possible. In the future, the company plans to use AI to spot support patterns that reveal product issues before they become ticket volume and to extend AI assistance thoughtfully into specialized and regulatory channels.

“Zendesk has driven our sustainable and secure growth by combining advanced AI capabilities, integration, and application-generation tools,” Caldas says. “It enables us to scale, predict, monitor, and evolve independently while meeting the financial market’s strictest compliance and cybersecurity requirements.”
