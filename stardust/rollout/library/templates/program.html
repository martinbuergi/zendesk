The 5 trends shaping the next era of CX

AI is changing how CX organizations operate, from the workforce they manage to the experiences they deliver. Explore the five shifts defining this next era of customer experience.

Trend 1: The AI Workforce Explosion

AI is expanding from individual use cases into a coordinated, intelligent workforce of specialized AI agents that are changing how work gets done at scale. 85% of leaders agree that winning companies in 2027 will orchestrate humans and AI agents most effectively.

Learn more

Trend 2: The Rise of Modular CX

CX is moving toward more configurable, specialized service. 84% of business leaders say AI workflow design is becoming a core CX operations skill. AI gives teams more control to match the right capabilities to each interaction and adjust service as customer needs change.

Learn more

Trend 3: The New Currency of AI Is Outcomes

AI success is shifting from the volume of work processed to the value of outcomes delivered. 85% of leaders agree that outcome-based pricing better aligns vendor incentives with resolved issues.

Leaders are looking closely at what their AI investment delivers, from resolution quality to customer and business outcomes.

Learn more

Trend 4: Trust and Security Move Into Every Interaction

As AI takes on more responsibility, trust can’t sit outside the experience. 87% of business leaders say verification must be embedded seamlessly in AI-led interactions. Verification, security, and accountability are becoming part of every AI-powered interaction.

Learn more

Trend 5: AI Becomes the New Customer Intermediary

AI is becoming a new gateway between customers and brands, shaping how people discover companies, get service, and decide when human expertise is needed. 80% of leaders agree that conversational AI will become a new gatekeeper between brands and customers.

Learn more

Frequently asked questions

What is the Zendesk CX Trends 2027 report about?

CX Trends 2027 gathers insights from 7,560 people to examine how AI is changing service experiences, and introduces the era of AI Management: a new way to direct intelligence to deliver better outcomes.

What does AI Management mean?

AI Management is the work of directing intelligence across people and AI agents: assigning work, coordinating systems, measuring quality and value, setting guardrails, and deciding when humans should step in.

Why should CX leaders read the Zendesk CX Trends 2027 report?

The report shows how AI is reshaping service experiences and the decisions leaders must make around workforce management, flexible operations, outcomes, security, and customer access.

What can CX leaders learn from the report?

CX leaders can learn how to manage AI more responsibly and effectively, design service experiences around their customers and business, and turn AI’s growing capabilities into a strategic advantage.

Trend 1

AI is becoming a workforce. Managing it is the new challenge.

AI agents are moving beyond individual tools to take on more specialized work across the service organization. As the AI workforce grows, organizations are beginning to rethink how work is assigned and coordinated.

That creates a new leadership challenge. 81% of business leaders say entirely new management structures are needed to oversee AI agents working across multiple departments. Leaders will need new approaches to ownership, coordination, and accountability.

Explore the full CX Trends 2027 report to see how the AI workforce is reshaping service.

Download the report

Trend 2

The future of CX is more configurable and specialized.

AI is giving CX teams more control over how service is designed and delivered. Instead of one model for every interaction, teams can combine agents, workflows, data, and human expertise based on the work at hand.

Within two years, 81% of business leaders say most service workflows will be built by operations teams rather than developers. As service becomes more modular, orchestration will be key.

Download the full CX Trends 2027 report to explore the shift toward Modular CX and what it means for the future of service.

Download the report

Trend 3

AI success is shifting from activity to outcomes.

As AI usage grows, leaders are looking beyond how much work it processes to what that work delivers. Resolution quality, customer impact, and business value are becoming more important measures of AI performance.

That shift is reflected in buying decisions too. 85% of business leaders agree that vendors unable to demonstrate clear ROI at the interaction level will lose contracts to those that can.

Download the full CX Trends 2027 report to explore why outcomes are becoming the new currency of AI.

Download the report

Trend 4

Trust must be built into the AI experience.

As AI takes on more decisions and actions, trust is becoming part of the service experience itself. Organizations need to balance stronger protection with a seamless experience, using the right data, permissions, and guardrails as AI takes on more responsibility. 78% of consumers expressed concern about AI-generated fraud.

Download the full CX Trends 2027 report to explore how trust and security are shaping the next era of service.

Download the report

Trend 5

AI is becoming the gateway to your brand.

As customers increasingly turn to AI to discover, compare, and resolve, brands will have fewer direct interactions with them. The companies that stand out will be those whose information and service experiences are accessible through the systems customers already rely on.

58% of consumers say a company’s service systems should work seamlessly with whatever AI assistant they already use.

Download the full CX Trends 2027 report to explore how AI is reshaping the customer-brand relationship and the future of service.
