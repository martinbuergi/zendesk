What is the Zendesk CX Trends 2027 report about?

CX Trends 2027 gathers insights from 7,560 people to examine how AI is changing service experiences, and introduces the era of AI Management: a new way to direct intelligence to deliver better outcomes.

What does AI Management mean?

AI Management is the work of directing intelligence across people and AI agents: assigning work, coordinating systems, measuring quality and value, setting guardrails, and deciding when humans should step in.

Why should CX leaders read the Zendesk CX Trends 2027 report?

The report shows how AI is reshaping service experiences and the decisions leaders must make around workforce management, flexible operations, outcomes, security, and customer access.

What can CX leaders learn from the report?

CX leaders can learn how to manage AI more responsibly and effectively, design service experiences around their customers and business, and turn AI’s growing capabilities into a strategic advantage.
