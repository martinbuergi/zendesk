Trend 1

AI is becoming a workforce. Managing it is the new challenge.

AI agents are moving beyond individual tools to take on more specialized work across the service organization. As the AI workforce grows, organizations are beginning to rethink how work is assigned and coordinated.

That creates a new leadership challenge. 81% of business leaders say entirely new management structures are needed to oversee AI agents working across multiple departments. Leaders will need new approaches to ownership, coordination, and accountability.

Trend 2

The future of CX is more configurable and specialized.

AI is giving CX teams more control over how service is designed and delivered. Instead of one model for every interaction, teams can combine agents, workflows, data, and human expertise based on the work at hand.

Within two years, 81% of business leaders say most service workflows will be built by operations teams rather than developers. As service becomes more modular, orchestration will be key.

Trend 3

AI success is shifting from activity to outcomes.

As AI usage grows, leaders are looking beyond how much work it processes to what that work delivers. Resolution quality, customer impact, and business value are becoming more important measures of AI performance.

That shift is reflected in buying decisions too. 85% of business leaders agree that vendors unable to demonstrate clear ROI at the interaction level will lose contracts to those that can.

Trend 4

Trust must be built into the AI experience.

As AI takes on more decisions and actions, trust is becoming part of the service experience itself. Organizations need to balance stronger protection with a seamless experience, using the right data, permissions, and guardrails as AI takes on more responsibility. 78% of consumers expressed concern about AI-generated fraud.

Trend 5

AI is becoming the gateway to your brand.

As customers increasingly turn to AI to discover, compare, and resolve, brands will have fewer direct interactions with them. The companies that stand out will be those whose information and service experiences are accessible through the systems customers already rely on.

58% of consumers say a company’s service systems should work seamlessly with whatever AI assistant they already use.
