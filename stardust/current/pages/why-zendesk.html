THE AI NETWORK EFFECT

Smarter service with every interaction

Customers and employees expect fast, accurate service across every channel. But most AI tools only solve part of the problem. They work in isolation, require constant retraining, and don’t connect to the broader service operation.

Zendesk takes a different approach with the “AI network effect.” Whether you're resolving tickets, spotting gaps with QA, or creating a knowledge base of easy-to-find answers with Zendesk Knowledge Builder, every interaction improves the next. That means fewer escalations, faster resolutions, and no added headcount. All on day one.

GUIDED

AI that acts, not just informs

Most tools give you data that confuses agents and creates more manual work for managers. Zendesk AI closes that gap with one connected system. AI agents resolve customer conversations end-to-end. Copilot guides human agents through every interaction in real time. QA scores every conversation automatically and flags what needs fixing. Analytics surfaces trends in plain language so managers can act quickly, not dig through dashboards.

That means fewer escalations, less repetitive work, and better coaching across the team.

UNIFIED

One platform. Zero silos.

With Zendesk, every channel, every agent type, and every operational function shares the same underlying data. AI agents, human agents, Copilot, QA, WFM, and knowledge all work together across email, chat, SMS, voice, WhatsApp, and social. No more losing context when a customer switches channels, or when an AI agent hands off to a human. Just one platform across CS, ES, and CC, so teams can consolidate tools, reduce complexity, and get a complete view of every interaction
