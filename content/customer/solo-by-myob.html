Lean team, big impact: Solo by MYOB's service-first playbook

MYOB launched Solo by MYOB to serve Australia’s underserved sole operators with an AI-first platform built from the ground up. Powered by Zendesk, Solo automates more than 90 percent of support interactions, delivers actionable customer insights, improves retention and satisfaction, reduces costs, and transforms service into a competitive growth advantage.

Operational savings reinvested in innovation

For decades MYOB has helped small businesses manage their finances. But when the company identified an opportunity to serve Australia’s 1.6 million sole operators, it became clear that this audience needed something fundamentally different.

“We really were looking at a whole new market for MYOB,” says Sally Davies, General Manager of Solo by MYOB. “Sole operators are a traditionally underserved market in Australia—and many don’t use accounting software.”

MYOB found that more than half of all sole operators still rely on spreadsheets to manage invoices, receipts, and taxes manually.

“They end up running back to what they’re comfortable with—and that means Excel spreadsheets,” Davies explains.

To break this cycle, MYOB decided to build a mobile-first platform designed to act as a digital sidekick for freelancers. The core philosophy was simple: customer service must be a part of the product itself, not a separate support layer.

AI-first support that scales with the customer

Delivering embedded customer service at scale required building the service platform around AI and automation from the ground up. Because financial services demand a high degree of trust, the system also had to balance automation with human expertise.

“We’re AI-first and digital-first—but not humanless,” says Davies. “There are moments where human support really matters, especially in financial services.”

When evaluating the market for a solution that could balance automation with a premium human experience, MYOB found Zendesk.

“Even though MYOB is 35 years old, we approached this as if we were a brand-new company,” says Davies. “And when we went to market to look at the different options available to us—Zendesk was the clear winner.”

Today, Zendesk AI agents provide instant, round-the-clock support to sole operators, using knowledge built from customer interactions and support content. Solo also uses Agent Copilot to help agents in areas where accuracy, compliance, judgment, or customer sensitivity require a human in the loop.

This strategy has delivered strong early results successfully automating the vast majority of inbound requests. Now, more than 90 percent of customer inquiries are now handled through automation support flows without requiring human intervention.

“We truly achieved this in Solo by MYOB using the Zendesk platform,” Davies says. “In 2025, we exceeded our subscription target by 45 percent.”

Turning insights into continuous improvement

With Zendesk at the core, Solo has built a connected service ecosystem that goes beyond traditional support. The team uses real-time behavioral insights to understand not just what customers are asking—but what they’re trying to achieve.

“With banking, we take compliance very seriously,” says Davies. “And there are really intentional moments where we put humans in the loop.”

Solo uses a combination of AI and human expertise to help the team identify opportunities to improve the customer experience. One example came when a community manager noticed customers struggling with open banking reauthorization.

“He saw that customers need to reconsent every 12 months in order to access their data,” Davies explains. “He asked himself, ‘How do I make sure that customers do that?’”

Using Zendesk App Builder—an AI-powered, no-code tool integrated into the Zendesk Admin Center—the community manager quickly built a visual account status indicator, helping customers take action before issues arise.

“He didn’t need a developer—he just built it,” Davies says. “With the app builder, we can quickly build things and start driving growth opportunities instead of just offering support.”

By proactively addressing the issues, what was once a leading support issue has now largely disappeared.

“That’s the power of closing the loop,” Davies says. “We’re not just answering questions—we’re removing the need for them.”

Driving growth through better experiences

Since launching, Solo by MYOB has proven that an AI-first customer experience drives customer acquisition and retention, reaching a 92 percent customer satisfaction score (CSAT).

“Were we surprised? Not so much,” says Davies. “We overachieved our acquisition numbers—and we massively overachieved our retention numbers. Customers are staying with us because of the way we’ve built the product—and we’re seeing that reflected in customer sentiment.”

By reducing contact rates and automating the majority of interactions, the team minimized operational overhead, freeing up resources to reinvest in product innovation.

“We have a small team,” says Davies. “We had planned to grow by a couple of headcount this year—but we just don’t need to. There’s $200,000 worth of savings that I can now put back into our product.”

Expanding what service can do

Moving forward, MYOB is continuing to push the boundaries of AI-powered service by exploring Zendesk’s AI voice agents and multilingual capabilities.

“There may be scenarios where our customers actually want to talk with someone,” says Davies. “They might be driving between jobs—and that feature would give us a different opportunity.”

This forward-looking strategy also includes supporting a growing base of entrepreneurs who are new to the country.

“One of our biggest growth areas is migrants who start their own businesses,” says Davies. “One of our challenges is providing support to them on how to use the product in a language that they understand more easily than English.”

But no matter what innovations are on the horizon, MYOB’s commitment to putting customers first remains constant.

“Service is not an afterthought,” says Davies. “And when you build service into the product from the beginning, it stops being a cost center and becomes a competitive advantage.”
