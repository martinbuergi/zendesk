The Smith Family builds trust with Zendesk Contact Center

For The Smith Family, every conversation matters. With Zendesk Contact Center, AI agents, and automation, the organization securely protects donor interactions, empowers agents with deeper customer insights, and delivers more personalized support. By strengthening supporter relationships, The Smith Family can focus on what matters most: creating more opportunities for children to succeed.

One in six young Australians grows up in a disadvantaged family. The Smith Family, Australia’s largest children’s education charity, exists to ensure poverty never stands in the way of a child’s education.

“We exist to support vulnerable Australians,” says Suzy Hilellis, Acting National Manager of Customer Experience and Strategy, Fundraising. “The service that we provide has to be really sensitive to their needs—it shouldn’t be shameful to ask for help.”

Delivering that experience means giving supporters confidence that every interaction is secure, seamless, and respectful.

“These families are experiencing a wide range of challenges,” Hilellis says. “Breaking that barrier so people feel comfortable asking for the help they need is really important.”

Turning every interaction into insight

When The Smith Family adopted Zendesk in 2018, its fundraising team managed customer emails through Outlook, relying on shared inboxes, folders, and manually maintained email templates. Its legacy phone system provided only basic call metrics.

“There was no reporting on why someone was calling in—it was all anecdotal,” Hilellis says. “We had no customer satisfaction metrics, and we had no way of knowing if what we were doing was right.”

Zendesk replaced fragmented processes with a single system, giving teams visibility into why supporters were reaching out—and how effectively those inquiries were being resolved.

“The biggest thing was actually being able to see what the inquiries are,” Hilellis says. “We can see whether our responses are effective—and we can continually improve everything.”

Protecting every donor interaction

Almost every donor interaction at The Smith Family involves sensitive financial information. As the organization expanded its fundraising operations, it needed a trusted way to process payments.

“We have to be PCI compliant,” Hilellis says of the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard, a set of rules established by major credit card companies. “We needed a more secure phone solution.”

To support its hybrid workforce while strengthening PCI compliance, The Smith Family adopted Zendesk Contact Center, built on the global infrastructure and security of Amazon Connect Customer.

“Our supporters are happy with it,” Hilellis says. “Rather than reading out their card information, they’re dialing it in. They feel like they’ve got more control.”

The migration proved remarkably seamless. Although the team anticipated some hiccups, they did not materialize.

“We were expecting the longer handle times and higher call abandonment rate to increase,” Hilellis says. “We’ve been really pleased that it hasn’t. Nothing’s fallen over. Everything was stable quickly.”

With every interaction managed in a single platform, agents can stay focused on conversations.

“Everything’s now in one system,” Hilellis says. “We actually get to see the supporter’s full history while we’re solving the inquiry.”

With better visibility into every interaction, The Smith Family can spend less time managing systems and more time strengthening supporter relationships. For the organization, reaching even a few more supporters means creating more opportunities for children to succeed.

“Getting through to even five percent more people can have a huge impact,” Hilellis says.

By making it easier for supporters to donate and for agents to focus on conversations instead of administration, The Smith Family can devote more energy to funding programs that help children succeed.

Raising satisfaction—not just speed—with Zendesk Copilot

Instead of replacing the human touch, AI helped agents personalize it. After introducing AI-powered Tone Shift and training agents to personalize responses, email customer satisfaction jumped from 86 to 94 percent in just one month.

AI-generated ticket summaries help agents quickly understand conversations without reading lengthy email threads or listening to call recordings. Topic detection helps teams prioritize sensitive inquiries, while AI Tone Shift enables agents to tailor responses to each supporter’s communication style.

As The Smith Family expanded its use of Zendesk AI and Copilot, success wasn’t measured in seconds saved—it was measured in better conversations.

“Being able to adjust the base template to the type of messaging that responds best to that customer has been really valuable,” Hilellis says. “You’re actually taking the time to understand what they’re trying to ask.”

For The Smith Family, the results reinforced AI’s greatest value: helping agents deliver more personalized, empathetic support.

Looking ahead

For Hilellis, Zendesk Contact Center is just the beginning. As The Smith Family explores AI, automation, and scalable quality assurance, the organization sees even greater opportunities to strengthen supporter relationships while reducing manual work.

Every improvement—from AI-powered personalization to automation—helps create more meaningful conversations with The Smith Family’s supporters and, ultimately, more opportunities for children to succeed.
