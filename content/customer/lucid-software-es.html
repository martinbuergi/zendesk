Lucid Software builds trust at scale with Zendesk for Employee Service

Lucid Software transformed employee support with Zendesk for Employee Service, replacing a chaotic shared inbox with a centralized, transparent experience for more than 1,000 employees. By combining self-service resources, automation, and knowledge management, Lucid reduced manual HR tickets by 30 percent, strengthened employee trust, and scaled support without dramatically increasing core People Ops headcount.

Lucid Software helps teams turn ideas into action. With products like Lucidchart for intelligent diagramming, Lucidspark for virtual whiteboarding, and airfocus by Lucid for product intelligence, organizations can visualize workflows, align teams around shared context, and accelerate execution in the AI era.

As Lucid continued to scale, it needed to ensure its internal operations could keep pace. With the company growing to more than 1,000 employees, maintaining a high-quality employee experience became more complex, especially for its lean People Operations team.

For Brittany Call, Vice President, People Operations at Lucid Software, maintaining trust at scale depended on employees getting the support they needed quickly, consistently, and with confidence.

But the team’s shared inbox, which worked when Lucid had just a few hundred employees, became a bottleneck. Employees lacked visibility into their requests, while the People Operations team spent valuable time managing repetitive questions.

“When I joined Lucid in 2018, we were about 350 employees and a newly formed HR team that was slogging through a single support inbox,” says Call. “While the team was incredibly scrappy, the process was fundamentally inefficient and left us with no way to measure our data or impact. We needed an internal system that could scale as fast as our headcount, moving us away from reactive firefighting and toward a proactive service model.”

Supporting an award-winning workplace

When employees can easily access support, they spend less time navigating administrative hurdles and more time focused on meaningful work.

“Our North Star is keeping the employee front and center as we design automated solutions,” says Call.

And when employees can easily communicate with their employer, they feel more confident that their issues will be resolved.

“Moving to Zendesk has deepened employee trust in our People Ops team,” Call says. “By removing the friction from getting help, we are signaling to our employees that their time is valuable and their questions genuinely matter.”

And Lucid’s employee-first approach has not gone unnoticed. It has been named one of Fortune’s Best Workplaces in Tech for six years running.

From a black box to a transparent employee experience

Zendesk helps provide a unified support experience across employee service channels.

“It provides a ‘one-stop shop’ for clarity across the organization–whether it’s HR, IT, or Legal—Zendesk acts as a centralized knowledge base for the entire company,” says Call.

Alongside ticketing workflows, Lucid built a robust knowledge base containing benefits information, policies, and program documentation. Employees can quickly find answers on their own, reducing friction and improving access to information.

Before Zendesk, the employee experience often felt like a black box.

“Employees didn’t really know exactly what to expect when they sent an email to People Ops,” says Call.

Now, with Zendesk for Employee Service, Lucid offers a transparent employee experience.

“Our employees know that they’re going to get great customer service,” Call says. “We’re going to respond quickly, and we’ll always have the content that they need.”

Scaling support without scaling headcount

Lucid has been able to nearly triple its overall headcount while keeping its core People Operations team incredibly lean.

“Zendesk allowed us to break the traditional model where internal support headcount must scale in lockstep with company growth,” says Call. “Staying lean means we can shift our budget and energy away from administrative overhead and reinvest directly into high-impact employee programs.”

Automation has played a major role in that success. By leveraging macros and standardized workflows, Lucid significantly reduced the volume of repetitive work reaching support teams.

“By doubling down on macros alone, we have been able to see a 30 percent reduction in our manual tickets,” says Call.

By eliminating repetitive requests, Lucid’s People Operations team can focus on higher-value work that requires empathy, context, and human connection.

“Nobody goes into HR thinking, ‘I want to answer the same question over and over again,’” Call says. “We’re here to help employees and drive a world-class employee experience. Zendesk frees us up to focus on meaningful human interactions and strategic work.”

An implementation that felt effortless

Lucid was already using Zendesk to support customers, so the transition to employee service was straightforward.

“The implementation was maybe the least interesting part of our story,” says Call. “It was just so easy and seamless.”

Because the platform is so intuitive and logical, the transition was seamless, allowing Lucid to focus on delivering value from day one.

From tickets to conversations

As employee expectations continue to evolve, Lucid sees artificial intelligence playing a growing role in delivering faster, more personalized support.

“The shift toward autonomous service is huge,” says Call. “The goal isn’t to replace the human touch, but to augment it.”

For Call, the future of employee service isn’t just about automating answers. It’s about making support feel more natural and accessible.

“The ultimate goal is frictionless, conversational support,” says Call. “Imagine an employee asking about a life event in Slack, and AI instantly summarizes the policy and walks them through the steps—only looping in our team when a human touch is required.”

Call envisions AI-powered experiences that bring together knowledge, context, and support directly within the tools that Lucid’s employees already use.

“It really would move us away from the experience of employees just submitting a ticket, into the realm of having an actual conversation,” Call says. “They can get the answers and solutions that they need—and remove the barriers to getting help.”
