TBAuctions speeds customer support with Zendesk Advanced AI

TBAuctions transformed customer service with Zendesk Advanced AI, reducing first response times from several minutes to 30 seconds while automating 70 percent of chat conversations. By resolving routine inquiries faster, the company improved customer experiences, empowered agents to focus on complex cases, and established a foundation for continued AI innovation across support channels.

For TBAuctions, speed is a competitive advantage.

Europe’s leading multi-brand digital auction platform helps B2B customers buy and sell used goods quickly and easily online. With Zendesk Advanced AI, TBAuctions reduced first response times from several minutes to just 30 seconds.

“From day one, we saw great results,” says Inge Vlam, Head of Customer Service, Auction Management & Facility at TBAuctions. “Our customers appreciate getting the correct answers much faster, while our agents can focus on the more challenging work. It’s really a win-win situation.”

Automating the questions customers ask most

When TBAuctions analyzed its support workload, the team found that many incoming requests were repetitive, making them ideal candidates for automation.

“At least 50 percent of the incoming questions were standard, easy-to-solve requests,” Vlam says. “Questions about pickup information, like ‘Can I change my pickup date?’ or ‘When is the pickup day?’ Those kinds of questions.”

TBAuctions deployed Zendesk Advanced AI for chat, automating high-volume inquiries using trusted knowledge from its Zendesk Help Center and website. Customers can now quickly resolve common questions without waiting for an agent.

As TBAuctions refined its AI agents, automated chat resolution increased from about 30 percent during the initial rollout to 70 percent. Chat now represents between 15 and 20 percent of customer interactions, up from roughly 10 percent before the AI rollout.

“We really wanted our chat response times to be perfect,” Vlam says. “If customers have to wait just as long as they would on the phone or through email—they won’t use chat.”

Helping agents focus where they add the most value

With Zendesk Advanced AI agents automating more than two-thirds of chats, agents can focus on the conversations that require human expertise.

“We wanted to make the work more challenging for our agents,” Vlam says. “Instead of answering the same standard questions over and over again, they can now focus on the cases where they really add value.”

The success of chat created a blueprint for expanding AI across additional support channels. Building on that success, TBAuctions expanded Zendesk Advanced AI to email, starting with routine customer inquiries while continuing to route more complex requests directly to specialist teams. Within the first few weeks, AI was already automatically resolving about 30 percent of eligible email requests while helping reduce response times.

Preparing human agents for AI

Like many organizations introducing AI, TBAuctions needed to address employees’ concerns about automation.

“We made it very clear from day one that we weren’t implementing AI to reduce our workforce,” Vlam says. “We wanted to make their work easier.”

Agents also played an active role in deciding which customer scenarios should be automated first.

“Because we brought them along on the whole journey, they really embraced the change.”

Building AI on a strong foundation

Before moving to Zendesk, TBAuctions struggled with a customer service platform that created multiple tickets from a single customer conversation. The additional tickets increased the volume of work for agents and made reporting more difficult.

“It happened that one actual customer question turned into four different tickets,” Vlam says. “That increased our workload and wasn’t efficient at all.”

After moving to Zendesk, TBAuctions discovered another advantage as it prepared to launch AI: its knowledge base was already in excellent shape.

“We were positively surprised by how good our knowledge base already was,” Vlam says. “We only needed to make a few small improvements.”

Accelerating AI adoption with Zendesk Professional Services

After successfully rolling out AI agents, TBAuctions turned its attention to continuous optimization. As Zendesk continued introducing new AI capabilities, TBAuctions wanted a faster way to put them into practice.

“I love how straightforward the communication is with the Zendesk team,” Vlam says. “It’s professional, practical, and easy to work with. They’re a great partner, and we get excellent support.”

The company had previously relied on an external implementation partner, but as its customer experience strategy evolved, it decided to work directly with Zendesk Professional Services through Zendesk Assist.

Working alongside Zendesk experts, TBAuctions adopted a train-the-trainer approach that helped build in-house expertise while continuously optimizing the platform.

“We had the right support from the Zendesk team,” Vlam says. “Within just a few months, we had implemented the main use cases.”

Professional Services also helped TBAuctions shift its focus from simply operating Zendesk to continuously optimizing it as new AI capabilities became available.

“Initially, we didn’t see the full potential of Zendesk,” Vlam says. “It was time to really work more closely with the team and implement those features.”

Expanding AI across the customer journey

For the next phase, TBAuctions plans to expand its use of Zendesk capabilities, including Quality Assurance, Workforce Management, Copilot, Knowledge Builder, AI-powered voice support, and Employee Service.

“We’re really embracing the new features of Zendesk,” Vlam says. “We can’t wait to see what comes next.”

With Zendesk Advanced AI, TBAuctions continues to deliver faster support for customers while giving agents more time to focus on the work that matters most.
