Serasa Experian expands digital customer service with Zendesk AI

A people-centered strategy is transforming the experience for consumers and businesses. Serving millions of people and companies across Brazil, Serasa Experian needed to advance its customer service strategy without compromising security, governance, or empathy. Over roughly six years of partnership with Zendesk, the company has built a digital transformation journey that now combines artificial intelligence, digital channels, and human support to deliver more efficient, personalized experiences.

Support that evolves with customers

Serasa Experian serves audiences with very different needs. On one side are consumers seeking help during difficult moments in their financial lives. On the other are businesses of all sizes, from small entrepreneurs to large organizations with complex processes.

This diversity called for a strategy that could provide consistent experiences, preserve customers’ freedom to choose their support channel, and ensure every interaction was secure, efficient, and relevant.

According to Renato Ciccarelli, Serasa Experian’s Customer Care Director, the transformation has always had a clear goal: to create digital journeys without losing the human side of the relationship.

“AI can help us accelerate not only productivity, but also humanization.”

A transformation built on trust

When Serasa Experian began modernizing, approximately 95 percent of its customer contacts came by phone, and only 5 percent by email.

As the company considered ways to support this evolution, information security, regulatory compliance, and data governance became decisive factors. As a company that works every day with sensitive financial information, finding a partner that could meet these requirements was essential.

The partnership brought new learnings over time. Ciccarelli says the process has involved continuous improvement and an ongoing exchange of knowledge. This approach allowed the operation to evolve gradually, adding new capabilities as its needs grew.

Artificial intelligence that scales while preserving empathy

As digital channels became more established, artificial intelligence began to play an increasingly important role.

In its consumer operation, Serasa Experian used AI to handle more than 500,000 support interactions in a single year, resolving many of them without transferring customers to a human agent.

For Diego Bueno, Serasa Experian’s Executive Customer Experience Manager, the goal was never to replace people, but to help them focus where they can add the most value.

“When we can use AI to increase scale, we can deliver the full potential we expect from the tool.”

Sentiment analysis during support interactions was another important capability.

At a Zendesk Showcase, the company shared the story of a customer who contacted Serasa Experian only to cancel two company user accounts after losing his wife and son in an accident. Recognizing the emotional context helped the agent handle the conversation with greater care. The customer later got back in touch to thank the company for the support he had received.

For Ciccarelli, the episode captures the company’s philosophy:

“Our challenge is person to person.”

Turning data into better experiences

In addition to automation, bringing B2B and B2C operations together on a single platform gave the company a more complete view of its customers.

Many consumers are also business owners. Bringing this information together in one environment made it possible to offer more consistent journeys, regardless of the channel or team responsible for support.

Another advance was the ability to guide each customer to the channel best suited to their needs. People less familiar with digital tools can still get voice support, while customers who are more self-sufficient can find quick resolutions through digital channels.

This approach helped reduce the repeat contact rate by more than 18 percent, reflecting greater accuracy in resolving cases.

According to Diego, personalizing the customer journey remains an ongoing effort, but the progress so far is already having a direct impact on the customer experience.

Ambition to keep evolving

Despite the results so far, Serasa Experian sees this transformation as a work in progress.

Teams are working to expand AI across different stages of customer support, so human agents can focus their efforts on more complex cases.

In B2B, the goal is to resolve 50 to 60 percent of incoming requests with AI. For Renato Ciccarelli, setting ambitious goals is part of the company’s culture.

“We have to learn to make a few good choices.”

Diego Bueno believes the speed of learning will continue to be a competitive advantage:

“Start fast, fail fast, and learn fast.”

With a strategy based on security, collaboration, and continuous innovation, Serasa Experian continues to expand its AI initiatives while strengthening a vision of customer service in which technology and empathy go hand in hand. Its partnership with Zendesk remains an important part of this evolution, helping the company build experiences that are simpler, more efficient, and more human.
