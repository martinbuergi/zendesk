How Motiva is building an omnichannel operation with Zendesk AI

With more than 51 million customers served across the highways and rail systems it manages, Motiva has placed customer experience at the center of its corporate strategy. By consolidating 101 support channels in Zendesk and implementing artificial intelligence to automate most interactions, the company is creating a more efficient operation that is prepared to scale and respond to the demands of a highly regulated industry.

When a customer focus guides strategy

Every interaction counts when millions of people depend on your services every day.

Motiva operates some of Latin America’s most important mobility infrastructure. From highways and subway lines to urban trains and light rail, the company maintains essential services that operate continuously, every day of the year. Approximately 51 million customers use its operations.

As its corporate strategy evolved, the company redefined its purpose as “improving people’s lives through mobility.” This change placed customers at the center of decision-making and prompted a company-wide reorganization, including the creation of a corporate Customer Experience function that reports directly to the CEO. The department reflects the need to deeply align business objectives with the daily needs of people who use the highways, train lines and subway systems. The model brings together communications, people and operations.

For Mauricio Tortosa, Motiva’s Director of Customer Experience, this structure ensures that customer experience is part of the organization’s strategic decisions. “The first advantage is for the customer. It’s about understanding the customer as a person—an individual who encountered a problem and is contacting the company to solve it.”

Direct contact with drivers and passengers generates a constant flow of valuable insights. Assessing 30 operational indicators across dozens of concessions requires a fully integrated management view.

The CX team serves as a center of excellence. While operations take place at the concession companies, the corporate team gathers insights through ongoing surveys and customer interactions across support channels. It turns these learnings into improvements and tracks implementation in partnership with operations, communications and people teams. This is Motiva’s Voice of the Customer program, developed together with Forrester Research.

A complex operation requires a single view

The scale of the operation made it clear that Motiva needed a platform capable of unifying information and processes.

Before beginning its most recent technology modernization, Motiva used a separate system to monitor social media interactions. The strategy called for expanding the platform to other channels, but the cost of doing so, combined with integration requirements, led the company to begin a new technology selection process.

Bringing information from different contact centers together in a unified interface was essential to the corporation’s financial and reputational health. Fragmented management made it difficult to monitor quality and respond quickly during the most sensitive customer interactions.

The thorough search for technological innovation was driven by a genuine desire to modernize processes safely. The rigorous process lasted four months, until the technical and commercial decision was officially finalized.

Technology to support people and decisions

The implementation was carefully planned before the project began.

The transition to the new technology was planned in great detail, with strict timelines for each concession. With support from implementation partner BCR, the project stayed within the expected budget and schedule. The new infrastructure organized incoming messages and calls across a single center for visibility and control.

The immediate change provided the structure needed to manage a huge volume of simultaneous contacts. The work involved consolidating 101 support channels and implementing 238 licenses in the first phase.

The immediate adoption of AI Agents enabled autonomous, humanized triage of lower-complexity requests. As a result, the automated service layer began handling most daily interactions with passengers and travelers, freeing human agents to manage more complex or critical situations where human intervention remains essential.

For human agents, Copilot became a valuable support resource, providing context, summaries and guidance during conversations.

The tool quickly scans historical data and presents essential context, allowing employees to focus on the most delicate details. “Copilot helps agents maintain consistency in the information, follow a script and receive a summary of what is happening with the customer,” Tortosa explains.

The solutions adopted also had a major impact on workforce management across the contact centers. Management can now clearly see temporary bottlenecks caused by unpredictable events or major national holidays.

Leaders have the right information to determine where to allocate staff and prevent unwanted delays across direct communication lines. This increased visibility has transformed how the company manages and resolves routine critical incidents.

According to Tortosa, all of this helps increase response consistency as the company continues to develop its tone of voice and operational maturity. “Zendesk helps us better understand what is happening throughout the customer service journey. Analyzing the complexity of our operation has become much easier, allowing us to act faster,” Tortosa says.

Greater visibility for faster action

Centralizing data changed how the team monitors operations.

The transition to an autonomous workflow positively changed the pace and distribution of work across the company. Simple, everyday questions stopped accumulating in waiting queues and began to be answered quickly during the first interaction by AI Agents.

The internal team gained the freedom and capacity to focus all its energy on cases requiring human sensitivity and empathy. The support environment became a place where professionals could deliver their full analytical and collaborative potential.

The volume of interactions resolved during the first contact quickly reached a remarkable success rate. “86 percent of the requests we receive today are being addressed by artificial intelligence,” Tortosa highlights. Human agents now dedicate their time to the 14 percent of situations considered critical or exceptional.

This segmentation has proven invaluable during highway incidents, where reduced time on the phone makes it possible to dispatch assistance quickly and save lives. The new unified control model has also significantly simplified reporting and accountability to regulatory agencies.

Improvements in resolution quality, measured by the new system, are now part of the criteria used to determine variable compensation for the corporate Customer Experience teams and the Customer Service teams supporting the Highway and Rail Platforms.

Complex traffic incidents have also lost their potential to congest the contact center’s phone lines. Customers who call simply to ask about highway traffic flow can get their answer and end the call within seconds.

The service has gained significant resilience in handling unpredictable, high-volume demand spikes.

Prepared to grow intelligently

The evolution of customer experience supports the company’s growth strategy.

Motiva plans to continue participating in new concession auctions. To do so, it is building an operation capable of quickly integrating new teams, channels and services while maintaining a consistent customer experience.

The company is also already looking ahead to the next stages of artificial intelligence. Priorities include specialized autonomous agents, continued workforce management improvements, ongoing service quality enhancements and expanding the knowledge base with AI support.

For Tortosa, the transformation driven by AI goes beyond customer service. “It’s not just changing customer service. It’s changing society.”

By consolidating an omnichannel operation and creating a structure designed to evolve continuously, Motiva is reinforcing its long-term vision. Throughout this process, Zendesk is becoming a strategic partner, supporting the company as it builds an experience that is scalable, consistent and ready for the future.
