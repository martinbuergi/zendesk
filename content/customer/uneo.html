Zendesk delivers a 360° customer experience for Unéo

With Zendesk, Unéo's call center agents have a complete 360-degree view of every member interaction from a single interface. The result is greater efficiency and higher customer satisfaction, with a CSAT score of 8.1 out of 10 and an NPS of +42.

Social protection for members of the armed forces may be a niche market, but it’s also highly competitive. As France’s leading military health insurer, Unéo knows that exceptional customer service is one of the best ways to stand out. Whether they are active service members or military retirees, Unéo members expect their insurer to provide the highest possible level of service.

Providing excellent customer service is a strategic priority for Unéo’s member services team, which handles approximately one million interactions every year across phone, email and mail. Whether responding to requests for certificates, reimbursements, benefit information or quotes—every inquiry must be handled efficiently and effectively.

Delivering simplicity and omnichannel service

To meet this challenge, Unéo implemented the Zendesk Enterprise Suite in 2020. Its top priority was adopting a customer service platform that would allow agents to manage every stage of a member’s request from a single interface.

“Before moving to the Zendesk Enterprise Suite, our teams had to use as many as seven different tools at the same time—which was extremely complicated,” François Lhermite, Director of Member Relations at Unéo, says. “Phone calls, email, forms, chat, messaging—with Zendesk, our agents now have a complete 360-degree view of every customer interaction. At a glance, they can see all the relevant information about a member’s policy on one screen. As a manager, I also use Zendesk Analytics every day to monitor ticket backlogs, SLA performance and individual productivity. It makes managing operations much easier.”

Flexible integrations

As an insurance provider serving military personnel, Unéo has unique operational and security requirements. The goal was to give call center agents a highly customized workspace tailored to those needs.

“We wanted to make the most of Zendesk’s integration capabilities,” Geanina Mantie, Project Director at Unéo, says. “Because we serve the armed forces, we operate under strict security requirements. For telephony, we chose Axialys, a Zendesk partner. Its cloud phone solution is hosted in Europe and automatically displays member records and creates tickets. For chat, we use chatbots from Tolk, an application available through the Zendesk Marketplace. We also integrated our document management system, allowing advisors to access all documents submitted by members via email or mail without ever leaving Zendesk.”

A phased rollout

Instead of deploying the Zendesk Enterprise Suite all at once, Unéo chose a phased rollout by department. The migration began in the summer of 2021 and was completed in the spring of 2022.

“Our front-office and back-office teams each have very specific responsibilities, so we wanted to avoid a big-bang rollout for all 300 users at once,” Mantie says. “Our priority was to establish a solid technical integration foundation while gradually deploying Zendesk and supporting employees as they learned the new platform.”

Zendesk’s flexibility also made the transition from the previous system much smoother.

“In addition to integrating our membership contribution management system, we connected our legacy CRM with Zendesk so departments that had not yet migrated could continue working without disruption,” Mantie says. “That preparation allowed us to thoroughly test and refine the solution.”

Throughout the project, Zendesk Professional Services played a critical role.

“The project team’s support significantly strengthened the expertise of Unéo’s functional administrators, which is a real advantage for us today,” Mantie says.

Rapid team adoption

The results were immediate. According to a recent internal survey, 94 percent of Unéo users reported being satisfied with the new platform.

“The teams embraced the new system very quickly. The 360-degree customer view has made handling customer requests much more efficient and seamless,” Sabrina Bourasseau, Call Center Manager at Unéo, says. “The platform is simple and intuitive. Since deploying Zendesk, we’ve seen a real shift in mindset. Our teams listen more closely to customers, go further in resolving issues and provide better advice. Improved information sharing has also helped break down silos across teams located in different offices.”

Members have benefited as well. Approximately 70 percent of requests are handled by phone, while 25 percent resolved by email.

“These usability improvements have translated into excellent customer satisfaction results,” Emmanuelle Godineau, Director of Customer and Employee Experience at Unéo, says. “We have achieved a CSAT score of 8.1 out of 10 and an NPS of +42—an increase of five points since deploying the solution. Today, 88 percent of email inquiries are resolved within 48 hours. While phone remains our primary support channel, email continues to grow rapidly.”

Looking ahead

Following the success of the implementation, Unéo plans to expand its use of Zendesk even further.

“To make our 360-degree customer view as complete as possible, we’ll continue incorporating feedback from our teams in the field,” Lhermite says. “Our teams are also exploring the creation of an internal knowledge base for employees. We will almost certainly test live chat, and we’re also considering expanding into new messaging channels such as WhatsApp, which is becoming increasingly popular, as a replacement for SMS. That would be a very natural evolution for communicating with our members, particularly when they are deployed abroad.”
