BetMGM Brasil doubles down on the player experience with Zendesk AI

BetMGM Brasil uses Zendesk AI Agents and AI Expert to automate 69 percent of player inquiries, improve conversation understanding from 92 to 97 percent, and deliver faster, more reliable support during peak sporting events while continuously optimizing performance through knowledge-base improvements, expert guidance, and ongoing collaboration across teams in Brazil.

When players have questions about their bet status or transaction, they expect answers immediately. For BetMGM Brasil, delivering that speed safeguards player loyalty and creates a true competitive advantage—and Zendesk AI Agents are making it possible.

“We invested in Zendesk AI Agents Advanced because of the customer experience,” says Barbara Esposito, Head of Operations at BETMGM Brasil. “It’s incredible. We’ve seen great results from the automation.”

Since implementing Zendesk AI agents, BetMGM has automated resolution for 69 percent of player interactions while increasing AI conversation understanding from 92 percent to 97 percent.

“But it wasn’t just about automation,” Esposito says. “When we looked at customer satisfaction, customers were happy with the answers they were getting from the AI.”

Operating with confidence during event-driven surges

Those gains proved especially valuable as BetMGM expanded in Brazil, where major sporting events can trigger dramatic, event-driven volume spikes.

Today, 98 percent of player conversations begin in live chat, where AI resolves questions about deposits, withdrawals, and the sportsbook using the company’s continuously evolving knowledge base.

“We just had the FIFA Club World Cup, and we could really see the difference AI made,” Esposito says. “During these events, support volume is insane. AI helped us significantly when we needed to update our processes.”

Building an AI-powered support operation

BetMGM launched operations in Brazil in 2025 with customer experience as a top priority. As the business grew, the company partnered with Zendesk AI Expert to build and continuously optimize its AI experience. The company also works closely with its Zendesk Premier teams to monitor performance and identify new optimization opportunities.

Instead of relying solely on hiring to keep pace with growth, BetMGM treated AI as a strategic initiative from the beginning.

“We had to hire and train a lot of people,” Esposito says. “It’s hard to maintain that as you grow.”

The team focused on building the right foundation for AI instead of simply deploying a chatbot.

“The success of the project came down to three things,” says Alberto Araujo, Quality and Training Leader at BetMGM Brasil. “A solid knowledge base, deep product expertise, and close collaboration between process management, quality, and training.”

To put that strategy into practice, BetMGM assigned an experienced support agent to lead the implementation. At the same time, the team rebuilt its knowledge base with AI-ready content that stays current as products and regulations evolve.

Turning expert guidance into measurable results

BetMGM credits much of its success to its partnership with Zendesk AI Expert.

“The big difference was counting on those experts to check everything we created before launching,” Esposito says.

Working alongside Zendesk’s local AI Expert team, BetMGM refined conversation flows and strengthened knowledge articles. The result was better AI performance across its Portuguese-language support operation.

“When the AI didn’t understand something, we just sent the customer to a human agent,” Esposito says. “A team member looked at that and said, ‘You’re not giving AI a chance to try again.’ Maybe the customer didn’t write a complete sentence. Maybe AI should ask another question first.”

The change delivered immediate results.

“We could see the difference right away,” Esposito says.

AI Expert also trained BetMGM’s team to manage and continuously improve the platform while providing a roadmap for future optimization. The roadmap also helped the team prioritize improvements and measure progress toward its goal of reaching 98 percent conversation understanding.

Creating a culture of continuous improvement

Instead of treating AI as a one-time deployment, BetMGM Brasil built a governed operating model for continuous improvement Support agents flag knowledge gaps in Slack, prompting quality, compliance, and legal teams to update content, ensure regulatory alignment, and improve AI performance.

“We update the information with compliance and legal teams,” Araujo says. “Then we measure the results and collect feedback.”

Next, the company plans to empower its teams further by expanding its use of Zendesk AI with Agent Copilot while continuing to enhance reporting and analytics.

For BetMGM, AI has become more than an automation tool. It’s helping the company deliver fast, reliable playerexperiences—whether it’s an ordinary day or the biggest sporting event of the year.

“Hopefully, AI will show us what needs to be improved in our processes and suggest new use cases,” Esposito says. “That’s what I want to see next.”
