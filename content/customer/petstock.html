Petstock modernizes telephony with Zendesk Contact Center

By connecting its existing Amazon Connect environment with Zendesk Contact Center, Petstock is reducing manual work, improving service levels, and giving agents more time to focus on customers and their pets.

Petstock operates more than 250 stores across Australia and New Zealand, alongside a growing e-commerce business. Its customer experience team supports customers, stores, customer and internal incidents, and loyalty activity across phone, messaging, email, web forms, and social media.

Telephony remains Petstock’s number-one customer contact channel. The team operates across two countries from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. with a relatively small team.

For Jasmine Taana, Project Manager in Petstock’s Customer Experience team, improving the agent and customer experience is personal.

“I started as an agent, talking to customers day in and day out,” Taana says. “I think I have a fundamental understanding of our customers and ways that we can try and improve their experience.”

When legacy technology started getting in the way

Before Zendesk Contact Center, Petstock relied on custom-built systems for telephony and customer support. While the systems functioned, they were difficult to upgrade, fix, and connect with other tools.

“We suffered from what we like to call legacy issues,” Taana says. “They worked, for the most part—but there was no growth. There was nowhere to go from there.”

The systems also created significant manual work. Agents had to listen back to calls, write detailed notes, search across systems, and complete follow-up work after the customer interaction had ended.

The problem became impossible to ignore when Petstock discovered that approximately 50–60 percent of calls were experiencing some form of error. The issues affected missed-call statistics, agent confidence, and the team’s ability to focus on customers.

“We realized we were having more issues with phone calls than actually just helping customers,” Taana says.

Why Petstock chose Zendesk Contact Center

Petstock assessed several vendors, but its decision was shaped by a combination of practical requirements and long-term ambition.

First, the team wanted to reduce the number of systems agents had to manage. Petstock already used Zendesk for other customer service workflows, so connecting telephony to the Zendesk environment offered a more consistent agent experience.

“We’re trying to centralize everything into one location,” Taana says. “If we can keep things in one place, it makes it a lot easier.”

Second, Petstock already had experience with Amazon Connect through its existing custom telephony environment. The implementation approach was to leverage that existing Amazon Connect environment within Zendesk, helping Petstock avoid starting from scratch.

Familiarity reduces the scale of the back-end change

“Being familiar with Amazon Web Services really did help us,” Taana says. “The original settings were still being used, so the change wasn’t quite as significant.”

Petstock also looked for a solution that could support its complex retail network. Quick Connects make it easier for agents to contact stores and internal teams, while routing flows direct calls to specialist groups such as equine support and store support.

The team also wanted a platform that could support future improvements in AI, omnichannel routing, reporting, and workforce management, not simply solve the immediate telephony problems.

What Zendesk Contact Center changed

Zendesk Contact Center is an AI-powered solution built on the Zendesk Resolution Platform that unifies voice, digital, and self-service channels in a single workspace, enabling AI and human agents to work together to resolve customer issues.

For Petstock, this meant connecting telephony with the customer service workflows agents already use in Zendesk. When a known customer calls, agents can see customer details, previous tickets, live transcripts, automatic summaries and resolution information.

“It takes a lot of the manual aspect out of what they’re doing,” Taana says. “With Contact Center, the customer details pop up straight away and our agents have a summary ready to go along with a live transcript.”

Petstock uses routing flows to direct calls to teams with the right knowledge. If a specialist team is busy, overflow routing allows the wider team to assist rather than leaving customers waiting.

For escalations, agents no longer need to write detailed minutes during the call. They can focus on listening and helping the customer, then use the transcript and summary to complete the escalation process afterwards.

“Now, our agents don’t have to do that,” Taana says. “They can focus on just helping the customer.”

A more supported implementation journey

The technology was only part of the change. Petstock also needed a reliable support model as it moved away from a custom-built environment.

Previously, the team often had to troubleshoot issues independently. Taana says Zendesk provides faster responses, clearer explanations, and a better understanding of the roadmap.

“At the moment, what we’ve got is a really fantastic team that replies super fast—sometimes within 10 minutes,” she says.

The implementation planning also identified opportunities to simplify flows, improve call transcription, configure resolution codes, strengthen reporting, and review callback and hours of operation functionality.

“Before Zendesk, we had nothing,” Taana says. “Now we’re getting updates every other day about cool new features that are coming.”

Improving service levels and agent efficiency

Petstock has seen several measurable improvements since implementing Zendesk Contact Center. Reported improvements include:

  • Call-related errors have dropped substantially
  • Service-level performance has improved by up to 15–20 percent
  • Customer satisfaction has improved, particularly after summaries and live transcripts were introduced
  • Resolution and follow-up times have improved
  • Agents can complete more work during the customer interaction

Reducing after-call work is particularly valuable for a small team operating across two countries.

“We used to have after-call work sitting at around three minutes, and now we’ve dropped down to 60 seconds,” Taana says.

A better experience for customers

The operational improvements are also changing customer conversations.

Taana says customers have noticed faster resolution times, while agents are better equipped to guide customers through issues without placing them on hold to search for information.

One interaction particularly stands out: an elderly customer who had contacted Petstock for years about her dog. After a recent conversation, she praised the team member for being attentive and remembering the details of the interaction.

The agent had been able to focus on listening rather than manually writing everything down.

“It’s nice to be able to see that there are some impacts like that,” Taana says.
