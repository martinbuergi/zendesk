Frequently asked questions

How are response times guaranteed under the Premier plan?

Premier customers receive priority routing with contractual, credit-backed SLAs. Issues verified as "Critical" receive a first response guarantee within 30 minutes, while "Major" technical blockages receive a guaranteed response within 1 hour, backed by global 24/7 coverage.

What are Break-fix zoom sessions?

A scheduled, direct support meeting between a customer and a designated Premier Support Engineer to diagnose, troubleshoot, and help resolve a specific issue. Break-fix sessions are conducted in English only.

How much do Zendesk Professional Services cost?

Zendesk Professional Services pricing is scoped based on the plan, the size of your environment, and the complexity of your requirements.

For an accurate quote tailored to your organization, contact your Zendesk account representative.

What are Zendesk Professional Services?

Zendesk Professional Services is a portfolio of expert-led engagements that helps businesses implement, configure, and continuously optimize their Zendesk environment so they can deliver better customer experiences faster.

From initial setup through long-term optimization, Professional Services pairs your team with Zendesk specialists who bring hands-on product expertise and real-world service best practices. Whether you're deploying Zendesk for the first time or scaling an existing environment, these services are designed to reduce time-to-value, increase ROI and help your team operate with confidence from day one.

How does Zendesk Suite support differ from Premier Plans support?

Zendesk offers two tiers of support coverage: Standard Support, included with all Zendesk Suite plans, and Premier Plans, built for customers who need 24/7 global coverage, faster response times, and proactive expert guidance.

Standard Support

All Zendesk customers have access to Standard Support, which provides essential coverage, including:

  • 8-to-5 Customer Care: Web and live phone support during local business hours, five days a week.

  • Help Center and Community Access: Self-service resources with product documentation, best practices, and peer discussions.

  • On-Demand Learning Courses: 75+ self-paced training courses to help your team get the most out of Zendesk.

  • Standard Support provides reliable assistance, with response times of approximately 30 minutes for business-critical issues and 24 to 48 hours for non-critical requests. It does not include direct access to Zendesk product experts or a dedicated success team.

Premier Plans Support

Premier Plans are designed for customers who need proactive, high-touch support and faster outcomes. Where Standard Support operates within local business hours, Premier enhances the experience through 24/7 global coverage and priority routing, so your team is never without expert assistance when it matters most.

Premier plan customers receive:

  • 24/7 Global Technical Support: Around-the-clock coverage across all regions.

  • Priority Routing: Faster handling for requests identified as critical or major.

  • Guaranteed Response Times and Follow-Ups: For major and critical tickets, with a credit-backed SLA guarantee.

What is included in Zendesk Launch, Assist, and Premier plans?

Zendesk offers tiered Professional Services plans so you can choose the level of support that matches your team's size, complexity, and goals.

Launch is the entry-level implementation offering, built for teams that need guided setup, core configuration, and foundational training to get up and running quickly.

Assist provides deeper, more hands-on engagement for organizations with more complex workflows, integrations, or customization requirements.

Standard support operates within local business hours for five days a week. Premier enhances the support experience through global technical support and priority routing, especially for requests that are identified as critical and major. Find out more about the Premier plans here.

Can Zendesk help with implementation and optimization?

Yes. Zendesk Professional Services supports you at every stage of your journey, from first deployment to long-term performance tuning.

During implementation, Zendesk specialists work alongside your team to configure your environment, define workflows, and set up integrations so your agents can start delivering support without unnecessary delays. After go-live, optimization engagements help you identify gaps in your setup, improve routing logic, refine reporting, and ensure your Zendesk instance keeps pace with your evolving business needs.
